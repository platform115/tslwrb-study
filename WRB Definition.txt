# WRB and WRB Hidden Gap (WHG) Identification Reference

## Purpose
This defines the rules for identifying Wide Range Bodies (WRBs) and
WRB Hidden Gaps on ES futures using M.A. Perry's WRB Analysis methodology.
It is the single source of truth for all WRB-related scripts, agents, and
notebooks in this project.

Source: M.A. Perry, WRBAnalysis.com — publicly documented methodology.
No rules in this document are invented. If a rule is not sourced from
Perry's documented work, it is marked [PROJECT CONVENTION] and explained.

---

## 1. Core Definitions

### 1.1 Candlestick Body
The body of a candlestick is defined as the absolute distance between
open and close prices, regardless of direction.

    body_size = abs(close - open)

This is NOT the total range (high - low). Wicks are excluded from body
measurement. This distinction is critical — a bar with a large total range
but small body is NOT a WRB.

### 1.2 Wide Range Body (WRB)
A bar is a WRB if its body is strictly larger than the bodies of each of
the three immediately preceding bars.

    is_wrb = (
        body[i] > body[i-1] AND
        body[i] > body[i-2] AND
        body[i] > body[i-3]
    )

Requirements:
- The WRB can be bullish (close > open) or bearish (close < open).
  Direction is recorded but does not affect WRB qualification.
- A doji (open == close, body = 0) cannot be a WRB.

### 1.3 WRB Direction
    bullish_wrb = is_wrb AND close > open
    bearish_wrb = is_wrb AND close < open

### 1.4 WRB Hidden Gap (WHG)
The WRB Hidden Gap is the price zone inside the WRB body that was NOT
traded through by ANY of the three prior bars.

Specifically: it is the region inside the WRB body that lies beyond the
highest high (for bullish) or lowest low (for bearish) of the three
prior bars' bodies.

For a BULLISH WRB:
    prior_body_high = max(max(open[i-1], close[i-1]),
                          max(open[i-2], close[i-2]),
                          max(open[i-3], close[i-3]))

    whg_bottom = prior_body_high
    whg_top    = close[i]          ← top of WRB body

    WHG exists if: whg_top > whg_bottom (there is unfilled space)
    WHG zone: [whg_bottom, whg_top]

For a BEARISH WRB:
    prior_body_low = min(min(open[i-1], close[i-1]),
                         min(open[i-2], close[i-2]),
                         min(open[i-3], close[i-3]))

    whg_top    = prior_body_low
    whg_bottom = close[i]          ← bottom of WRB body

    WHG exists if: whg_bottom < whg_top (there is unfilled space)
    WHG zone: [whg_bottom, whg_top]

A WRB does not always produce a WHG. A WRB without a WHG is still a WRB.
A WHG cannot exist without a WRB.

### 1.5 WHG Fill Status
A WHG is considered FILLED when subsequent price action trades completely
through the WHG zone — meaning a bar's range (high to low, including wicks)
covers the entire zone from whg_bottom to whg_top.

    filled = (subsequent_bar.high >= whg_top AND
              subsequent_bar.low  <= whg_bottom)

[PROJECT CONVENTION] Fill status is tracked as:
    UNFILLED  — no subsequent bar has traded through the full zone
    PARTIAL   — price has entered the zone but not fully covered it
    FILLED    — price has fully covered the zone

## 3. Output Schema ideas which might be of use


    ts_event        datetime UTC    bar timestamp
    session_date    date            trading date (CT)
    symbol          str             front-month contract
    timeframe       str             "30m", "15m", etc.
    open            float           bar open
    high            float           bar high
    low             float           bar low
    close           float           bar close
    volume          int             bar volume
    body_size       float           abs(close - open)
    prior_body_max  float           max body of prior 3 bars
    direction       str             "bullish" | "bearish"
    has_whg         bool            True if WHG exists
    whg_top         float | None    top of WHG zone
    whg_bottom      float | None    bottom of WHG zone
    whg_size        float | None    whg_top - whg_bottom
    whg_fill_status str | None      "UNFILLED" | "PARTIAL" | "FILLED"
    is_roll_day     bool            True if bar is on a contract roll date
    session         str             always "RTH" in this output

## 4. Known Edge Cases

### Roll Day WRBs
Bars where is_roll_day=True may show anomalous body sizes due to the
price gap between contracts at roll. These are valid bars in the data
but should be flagged and reviewed / categorised separately in backtesting.

### Identical Body Sizes
If body[i] == body[i-1], the bar does NOT qualify as a WRB.
The condition is strictly greater than (>), not greater than or equal (>=).

### Zero-Body Bars (Doji)
body_size == 0 cannot be a WRB. These bars are skipped in identification.
They can appear in the prior 3 bars without issue.

## 5. Targets - WRB pt1m pt2, and pt3
WRB pt1 is defined as the WRB which occurs in the direction of the trade after the trade has been entered.
pt1 = the first 1m WRB in the trade's direction (bullish=long, bearish=short) with ts_event ≥ entry-minute (entry bar may qualify). It completes at ts_event + 60s. A trade is target_reached if still open at pt1 completion, else failed_trades. (Also update WRB_Definition.txt §5 itself — it's the single source for WRB defs.)
